Cookie Policy

Effective Date: Feb 25, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how EdgeAlphix LLC ("EdgeAlphix," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, unless you disable them through your browser settings or available preference tools.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve user experience, and provide information about site usage.

Cookies may be temporary (session cookies) or persistent (stored until deleted or expired).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. They may:

  • Enable navigation and page rendering
  • Maintain security and session integrity
  • Support basic performance and infrastructure stability

These cookies cannot be disabled without affecting website functionality.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with the website. These cookies may collect:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referring websites
  • Browser and device information

This information is used in aggregated form to improve website performance and reliability.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies may remember user preferences, such as interface settings or consent choices, to improve usability.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services used for:

  • Content delivery
  • Website analytics
  • Infrastructure monitoring

These third parties may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through:

  • Your browser settings
  • Built-in cookie preference tools (if available on our website)

Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact website functionality.

5. Do Not Sell or Share

EdgeAlphix does not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

Cookies used on our website are intended for operational, performance, and security purposes.
